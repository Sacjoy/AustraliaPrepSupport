Lifetime Access & Purchases

Lifetime Access is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. It is not a subscription and does not renew.

If you previously purchased Lifetime Access using the same Apple Account, open the app and select Restore Purchases.

Practice Questions & Study Content

If premium questions or study content do not load, confirm that your device has an internet connection, reopen the app, and try again. If the issue continues, contact support using the email above.
